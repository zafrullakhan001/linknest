---
description: Verify Tab Scheduling Fix
---

1. Open a tile in Edit mode.
2. Click the calendar icon on a tab.
3. Set a "Visible From" date in the future (e.g., tomorrow).
4. Click "Done" and then "Save Changes".
5. Verify that the tab disappears from the view mode (because it's scheduled for the future).
6. Refresh the page.
7. Verify that the tab is still hidden.
8. Enter Edit mode again.
9. Verify that the tab is visible in Edit mode and has the "Scheduled" badge.
10. Open the scheduling dialog and verify the date is still set.
11. Clear the date and save.
12. Verify the tab is visible again in view mode.
